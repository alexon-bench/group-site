96- or 384-Well Plate Heatmap Generator

Load a statistics CSV from your analysis software (i.e. Floreada.ioa>, choose a variable, and see it colored across a 96-well or 384-well plate with each sample name on its well. Your file is read in this browser only and is never uploaded.

Data
Plate

Changing these settings rebuilds the layout and clears manual edits. Drag samples between wells, or select a well to pick its sample from a list.

Normalize

While picking, click wells to mark or unmark them, or click a row letter or column number to toggle that whole line. Several reference wells are averaged for each variable. The mark follows the sample if you move it.

Colors

Pick a fluorophore to make its color the maximum, or change either color to build your own scale. The PNG uses the same colors.

Well labels
Save

Files are created in your browser and saved through your normal download prompt. Nothing is stored between visits.
