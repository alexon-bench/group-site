Random DNA Sequence Generator

Generate one or more random DNA sequences at a chosen length and GC content.

Bases are placed to hit this GC% as closely as integer rounding allows, then shuffled into random order.

All processing happens locally in this browser tab using JavaScript, and sequences are built with the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator. Nothing you enter is stored, uploaded, or sent anywhere — results disappear when you leave or reload the page.
