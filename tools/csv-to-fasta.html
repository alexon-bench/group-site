CSV to FASTA

Convert a CSV file, or a pasted table, of name and sequence values into a FASTA file. Everything runs in your browser; no file ever leaves your device.

The file must have a header row with columns named "name" and "sequence" (any column order, case-insensitive).

or

You can also paste a table copied from a spreadsheet (tab-separated), with or without a "name" / "sequence" header row.

Storage: This tool does not use cookies, local storage, or any network requests. Your CSV and the generated FASTA text exist only in this browser tab and are cleared when you reload or close the page.
