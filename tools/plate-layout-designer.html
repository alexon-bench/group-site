Plate Layout Designer

List your samples, give each one optional conditions (e.g. "with target, without target") and a replicate count, and set an optional dilution series that applies across the whole plate. This tool lays every well out automatically — replicates stacked in a column, dilution steps spread across a row — onto a 96- or 384-well plate (spilling onto extra plates if needed), and lets you export the finished well map as a CSV.

Plate & defaults

These apply to every sample row below unless a row overrides the replicate count.

Comma-separated labels, one per step. Avoid commas within a label (e.g. use "1:1000", not "1:1,000") since commas separate the steps. Leave blank if samples don't need this. Each row below can opt out with its checkbox.
Off by default: wells are packed to fill each plate completely, which may split a sample's replicate/treatment wells across two plates. Turn this on to keep every sample/condition's wells together as one block — this can leave some wells unused per plate when block shapes don't divide the plate evenly.
