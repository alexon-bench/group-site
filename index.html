Make something together

Bring the question. We’ll help with the rest.

A tool can begin as a sketch, a repeated task, or a wish that the computer handled this part. Use any assistant or editor you trust; the MTech brief keeps each experiment self-contained and the shared site steady.

  1. 1
    Share the MTech brief

    Give the guide to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or another builder, then describe the question you want to explore.

  2. 2
    Shape one focused tool

    Everything lives in one understandable HTML file, with room to experiment and clear boundaries around the rest of the site.

  3. 3
    Pass it along

    Send the finished file, its title, and a one-sentence note to the site maintainer. Git experience is entirely optional.
